# Strand: astro-science

**What a year of urban drift-scan reveals — the science atop the astro pipeline,
and the sampling theory that makes it possible.**

The **science / insight** strand of the astro estate: the long-horizon question
everything else scaffolds — *what does a year-scale, long-baseline urban
astronomy dataset yield?* — and the connective theory that answers it. It is a
**development** strand (an open research frontier, STATE is a moving frontier),
above the per-camera keepers (astro-polecam, astro-eclipticam, astro-canon) and
the data keeper (astro-storage). It does not own capture or plumbing; it owns
the *ideas that turn accumulated frames into named, measured stars*, and the
**public face** those results reach (the `/astro` web deliverables).

Consolidates the theory that used to be scattered across four strands
(2026-08-02): **astro-subpixel** (PSF/undersampling, sub-pixel information,
dither mechanisms), **astro-breathing** (VCM focus-breathing as a sampling
lever), the **theory half of astro-storage-discussion** (the accumulation
capacity law + TDI/drizzle), the **sidereal-space static accumulator** direction
(from astro-v3s), and **astro-deliverables** (the publish/website output end).
Spans `~/astro` (methods, pipeline) and `~/mywebsite` (the deliverables site).
Its sibling **astro-storage** owns the storage *engineering*; this strand owns
the *science*.

## Session ritual

1. Import spooled ideas with `idea --import`, then read
   `STATE.md` (current state, decisions) and `IDEAS.md` (inbox).
2. Triage new ideas with Peter: promote to STATE.md pending list, or drop.
   Delete triaged entries from IDEAS.md.
3. Work. Commits go to the repo the change belongs to — this strand dir
   holds only curation files.
4. Session end (or on `dcp`): update STATE.md — what changed, what's
   pending, decisions made. Keep it curated prose, not a log.
